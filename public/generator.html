Lagos State Scheme of Work

For First Term, the uploaded school scheme is the authoritative source for KG 1, KG 2, Nursery 1, Nursery 2 and Primary 1–Primary 5. Volume 1 is mapped to KG 1, Volume 2 to KG 2, Volume 3 to Nursery 1 and Volume 4 to Nursery 2 because their topics show the progression in those classes. The separately labelled Primary 1–Primary 5 tables remain mapped to their named classes. The AI expands the exact selected topic into the DAFS lesson-note format and creates objectives when the PDF does not explicitly list them. Second/Third Term continue to use the existing catalog until the school supplies those schemes.

DAFS lesson-note format

The generated note follows the handwritten example supplied by the school. The AI writes the actual teaching content for the selected topic (not a description of what the teacher should do): Name, Class, Date, Subject, Week, Topic, Duration, Learning Objectives, Instructional Materials, Reference Materials, detailed Lesson Content, step-by-step Presentation with Teacher/Pupil Activities, Evaluation, Conclusion and Assignment.

Curriculum basis: Lagos State scheme entries loaded into the school application, supplemented by teacher-entered official scheme information when an exact catalog entry is not available. The AI must never invent or falsely label missing Lagos State curriculum data.
